##### Abstract:
This paper provides direct evidence on how liquidity constraints affect job search behaviour. We combine data on job applications from Danish UI recipients with detailed individual balance sheet data, allowing us to construct measures of liquidity at the onset of unemployment and examine how it shapes the types of jobs targeted. We build and study several relevant characteristics of applied for jobs, e.g. the typical duration and wages. To address endogeneity concerns in liquidity constraint status, we additionally exploit variation arising from different types of household wealth shocks in the period leading up to unemployment entry.
